## About
- About **1.5 acres**; Chinese fishing nets visible from the island
- Name: **Island D Cochin**; tagline: **An Island to yourself**
- Why choose us: *"Why hire a party hall when you can hire out a party island just for you and your loved ones?"*
- Story: bought by **Pazhampilly Thomas** in the **1960s** as a private recreational space and farm for his family. Passed to his son **Denny Thomas**, who turned it into a resort and event destination in **1994**. Then known as **Sandalwood Island**; drew visitors from around the world, including international guests and celebrities; hired by hospitality groups including **Taj** and **CGH Earth** for private experiences and events. Later renamed Island D Cochin
- Positioning: a private island **event and day-out destination** — premium, peaceful, exclusive
